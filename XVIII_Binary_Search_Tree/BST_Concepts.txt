1. BINARY SEARCH TREE:
=====================
- For every node, all the elements on its left are smaller and
  all the elements on its right are larger.

                30

    10      20      40      60

- It is useful for searching.
- It will not have duplicates.
- Inorder traversal of BST will give you elements in sorted order.

2. DRAWBACKS:
=================
The drawback of BST is that its hieght can not be controlled.
TC varies from log(n) to n, depending on the order of insertion

Case 1:
+++++++
Given Keys:
40  20  30  60  50  10  70

                    40 
            20              60
        10      30      50      70

height = 2


Case 2:
+++++++
Given Keys:
10  20  30  40  50  60  70

    10 
        20 
            30
                40
                    50
                        60 
                            70

height = 6

TC depends on the order of insertion which we can not control.
Min TC(BST) = O(log n)
Max TC(BST) = O(n)


Solution to above Problem:
++++++++++++++++++++++++++++
Height balanced binary search trees --> AVL Tree
